Common Questions

How do I get to Taniti?

Most visitors fly in, and a small cruise ship docks weekly. Without a car, you can use buses, taxis, bikes or your feet. Taniti observes national holidays, and some shops close on those days.

What currency is used on Taniti?
The U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, several banks exchange currency, and many accept major credit cards.
What are the power outlets like?
120 volts, the same as in the United States.
What if I need a doctor?
There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
Can I get around without a car?
Yes. Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., private buses serve the rest of the island, and taxis are available in Taniti City.
Are attractions open on holidays?
Many attractions and restaurants close on Taniti’s national holidays, so plan ahead.
Is English spoken?
Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.
When can I buy alcohol?
Alcohol can’t be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m.
Is Taniti safe?
Violent crime is very rare, but as tourism grows there are more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crime.
