Things to do

Food

Saltwater Bistro – Fine dining restaurant

From quick bites and late-night grocery runs to high-end dining, Taniti has it all. Explore some of our featured food offerings on the island.

Rainforest & Volcano

Mount Kalo Trek & Canopy Tours

Immerse yourself in the island’s lush interior. Explore cascading waterfalls, active volcano viewpoints, and local wildlife with our expert guides.

Beaches

Taniti City Beach – Family-friendly

Explore a striking variety of coastal landscapes, from rugged rocky shores to the pristine white sands that gracefully encircle Yellow Leaf Bay.
