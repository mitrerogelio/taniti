Welcome to Taniti

An unforgettable journey awaits

Spend your days on the white-sand beaches of Yellow Leaf Bay, hiking through lush rainforest, or visiting Taniti’s active volcano. Snorkel, zip-line, or tour the island by boat or bus, then explore the shops, pubs, and nightlife of Merriton Landing. Whether you’re here with family or on a honeymoon, there’s plenty to fill every day of your stay.
