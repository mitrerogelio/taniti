Lodging at Taniti

From hostels to a four-star resort

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging, from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts, all regulated and inspected by the Tanitian government.
